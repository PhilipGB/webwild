# Asset provenance and permitted use

The modular bridge GLB meshes, Godot wrappers, example assembly and generated previews are **original assets created for this Webwild handoff**. They are not downloads from a third-party marketplace and are not replicas of commercial models. They use only original geometry, vertex colours and freely available procedural construction, without embedded fonts, images or third-party textures.

You may copy, modify, adapt, redistribute and use these assets in Webwild, including private and commercial builds. The optional generator script is provided for reproducibility and may be modified with the project. No separate attribution is required, although retaining this provenance note is useful for documentation.

The existing Webwild project and its other assets retain their own applicable licences. The example 4 m assembly is reference art; do not mistake it for a fully functional game bridge.
