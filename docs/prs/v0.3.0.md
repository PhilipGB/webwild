## P0 — Scalable world contracts, persistence foundation

Status: **DONE** (no new terrain yet, by design).

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p0-world-foundations`:

- `src/Core/WorldCoordinates.cs` — signed 64-bit `ChunkCoordinate` (24 m chunks,
  13-point 2 m terrain grid), `WorldPos` (chunk + local doubles, floor
  semantics symmetric across zero), `WorldOrigin` origin rebasing.
- `src/Core/TerrainSampler.cs` — seeded coordinate-based value noise
  (`DeterministicHash`, SplitMix64) that is continuous across chunk boundaries
  and identical on regeneration.
- `src/Core/ChunkData.cs` — engine-free chunk payload (height grid, prop
  placements) with a bit-exact `ContentHash()`.
- `src/Core/LandmarkCatalog.cs` — constant authored-landmark origins
  (garden at world origin; Reedwater at (96, 0, 0)); these are a save contract.
- `src/Core/WorldManifest.cs` — world-manifest schema 4 (seed, player `WorldPos`,
  landmark visits, exploration record, embedded schema-3 colony payload) plus
  versioned `ChunkChangeSave` records (built webs, food state, markers).
- `src/Core/WorldSaveStore.cs` — durable manifest + per-chunk files with
  atomic writes, `.bak` rotation, interrupted-temp-file cleanup and
  corrupt-record tolerance (regeneratable baseline is never saved).
- `src/Core/WorldSaveMigrator.cs` — legacy schema 1–3 → manifest wrapping with
  exactly one semantic change (player position becomes a world position);
  reverse extraction restores the established simulation load path.
- `docs/adr/0001-never-ending-world.md` — decision record covering world
  coordinates, chunk identity, determinism, landmarks, streaming, saves,
  navigation, off-screen simulation, regeneration and migration.

## P2 — Seamless world, schema-4 manifest saves

Status: **DONE** — Reedwater Bank is embedded in the continuous world and
reached on foot; new saves are written as schema-4 world manifests.

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p2-seamless-world`:

- **One continuous world**: Reedwater Bank is instantiated at its constant
  world origin (96, 0) and stays resident alongside the garden. Both authored
  landmarks are visible, collidable and streamed around simultaneously; the
  connecting meadow is ordinary generated ground.
- **Walking replaces travel**: crossing a landmark's walkable rect on foot
  activates its missions, helpers, HUD and interaction anchors
  (`GameSimulation.EnterLandmark`). The travel dialog, travel labels and
  teleport path are removed from normal play.
- **Physical unlock**: a bramble gate seals Reedwater's west wall until the
  garden story completes. Unlocking removes the barrier's collision and mesh;
  the zone change is never refused, so gating is expressed by the world.
- **Authored integration**: Reedwater gains a west gate, gate landing and
  chunk-aligned ground skirts; `WorldGenerator` excludes both landmark
  footprints and blends heights to the authored level around each. Reedwater
  keeps its local fill lights but shares the garden's sky and sun, so the
  continuous world has one lighting environment.
- **`WorldSaveService`** (`src/Core/WorldSaveService.cs`) is the live save
  path: it writes schema-4 manifests through `WorldSaveStore`, migrates legacy
  schema-3 saves on first load (keeping the legacy file as rollback), preserves
  the New-Garden archive as a durable manifest copy, and never falls back to
  legacy data when a manifest exists but is corrupt.
- **Per-save world seeds**: one seed drives both the colony simulation and
  terrain generation, is stored in the manifest and restored on load. Probe
  runs keep the historical fixed seed for deterministic artifacts.
- Save validation accepts seamless exploration positions in either landmark
  frame; authored worker positions remain tight.

## P3 — Biomes, landmark aprons and origin rebasing

Status: **DONE** — the procedural meadow has three distinct biomes, the
authored Reedwater edge is feathered, and travel stays precise indefinitely.

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p3-biomes-movement`:

- **`src/Core/BiomeMap.cs`** — deterministic three-biome field. Two
  low-frequency noise channels select moss and bloom; the remainder is open
  meadow, and the weights always sum to one so borders are soft gradients.
  Moss sinks gently (−0.55 m) and bloom rises (+0.85 m) with capped slopes.
  `TerrainPalette` owns the shared colours and deterministic per-quad tint.
- **Chunk content** — `ChunkData.Biomes` stores the mix at every grid point,
  follows it through `ContentHash`, and `ChunkTerrainBuilder` blends per-quad
  biome colours with the low-poly patchwork tint.
- **Biome dressing** — moss chunks favour ferns, moss patches, mushrooms,
  stones and logs; bloom chunks favour flowers and tall grass; meadow keeps
  the authored garden mix. Dressing density roughly doubled from P1.
- **Reedwater aprons** — the chunk-aligned ground skirts are covered with
  generated-style per-quad meshes under the streaming host; the authored box
  tops are hidden while their collision stays authoritative. This removes the
  flat uniform skirt tops at the landmark seam.
- **Origin rebasing** — presentation coordinates rebase at a 16 km threshold
  on a 64 m grid. The logical world, saves, simulation, streaming and probe
  coordinates are unchanged; camera, physics, chunk host, landmark roots,
  view hosts and world labels move together. A far teleport rebases
  immediately, and a reload rebases from the saved logical position.
- **Wilderness HUD** — outside landmark rects the location label reads
  "Wild Meadows" and the status names the current biome; entering a landmark
  restores that landmark's labels and hints.
- **Probe surface** — biome name/weight lookup, deep-core biome discovery,
  generated height, loaded prop count, logical player position, engine
  position, origin offset and camera pitch framing.
- Save schema unchanged from P2 (schema 4); biome data regenerates from the
  saved world seed.

## P4 — Rivers and generated crossings

Status: **DONE** — the procedural meadow now has carved, hazardous rivers and
stable walkable crossings.

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p4-rivers-bridges`:

- **`src/Core/RiverMap.cs`** — deterministic meandering east-west rivers every
  260 m. Each band has a varying width, a flat channel bed, a 4.5 m tapered
  bank and a water level that follows the local landscape instead of forming
  an impossible flat plane. Sampling is a pure function of seed and position,
  so neighboring chunks, unload/reload and different sessions agree exactly.
  Authored landmark rects are excluded.
- **Water presentation** — `ChunkData.WaterQuads` stores the water surface
  owned by each chunk with per-corner levels. `ChunkTerrainBuilder` builds a
  translucent teal surface and tints the wet riverbed and bank with the shared
  terrain palette, so the water reads as a carved channel through the low-poly
  ground. Water is visual only; the carved terrain collision and a pure hazard
  field govern gameplay.
- **Crossings** — `RiverMap.BridgesForChunk` generates a stable low wooden
  bridge every 150 m along each river; the first is at x = 0 on the river south
  of the garden. Each bridge is owned by exactly one chunk. The deck is a solid
  tilted box meeting both sampled banks, with a minimum water clearance,
  visible planks, side beams, support legs and woven-silk rails. The bridge ID
  and geometry regenerate identically after unload.
- **Navigation and recovery** — entering a river's hazard mask below water
  height recovers the spider to the last safe bank position, with guidance to
  seek an old silk crossing. The toast re-arms only once the player is clear of
  the channel. Saves written before rivers existed that now resolve to deep
  water are moved to the nearest dry bank on load, and the remembered position
  is updated so the next save is safe.
- **Prop placement fix** — procedural props are now positioned in chunk-local
  coordinates. Before this phase every prop was offset by its chunk origin,
  which scattered dressing one chunk over and made close-up meadows look
  sparse. Core placements remain world-space; only the Godot child transform is
  corrected.
- **Probe surface** — river sampling, horizontal mask, height-aware danger,
  crossing lookup, loaded water-quad count, bridge residency and a
  prop-containment contract.

## P6 — Wild food and regeneration

Status: **DONE** — the endless meadow now supplies the colony with persistent,
regenerating wild food stored in per-chunk records.

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p6-food-resources`:

- **`src/Core/FoodSources.cs`** — `FoodSourceDefinition` with stable world IDs
  (`food:<chunk>:<slot>:<kind>`), a runtime `FoodSourceState` carrying
  `AvailableAtTick`, and `FoodStateSnapshot` for save grouping.
- **Deterministic generation** — three candidate slots per generated chunk
  produce berries, mushrooms or sunflower seed heads on dry land; authored
  footprints and water are excluded. Content participates in `ContentHash`.
- **Tick-based harvesting** — `GameSimulation.TryHarvestFood` grants the
  source's food once with a ledger key containing the source ID and absolute
  tick, then stores the next availability tick. Regeneration follows the
  simulation clock whether or not the chunk is loaded; expired states are
  pruned instead of persisted.
- **`scripts/actors/FoodView.cs`** — the generated model plus a warm ripe glow,
  a small sprout during regrowth, and a nearby "E · forage …" or
  "… regrow in Ns" prompt. Availability is read from the simulation only.
- **Interaction and HUD** — E forages the nearest ripe source with a toast,
  collect sound and context line; help text explains wild food.
- **Per-chunk persistence** — only depleted sources are written to the owning
  chunk's `ChunkChangeSave.FoodSources`. Untouched chunk records are preserved,
  known chunks whose last food change regrew are cleaned up, and records carry
  the world seed.
- **New-Garden isolation** — `WorldSaveService.ArchiveExisting` moves live
  chunk records into `previous-chunks/`, so old depletion cannot leak into a
  fresh world. Archive metadata keeps colony progress; wild food baseline
  regenerates when a previous garden is restored.
- **Streaming integration** — `ChunkStreamer` exposes loaded `ChunkData`; the
  adapter imports a chunk's persisted food state before creating views and
  removes those views when the chunk unloads.

## P7 — Physical worker navigation

Status: **DONE** — helpers walk between landmarks and across generated river
bridges instead of teleporting with the colony.

Delivered on `feat/v0.3-p7-worker-navigation`:

- **`src/Core/WorkerNavigator.cs`** — hierarchical world navigation: landmark
  hedge-gate waypoints, a coarse corridor search over generated river crossings,
  and terrain-followed local legs. It never routes through deep water or authored
  walls.
- **Physical journeys** — `MoveColonyTo` starts each helper on a route from its
  current world position to the destination nursery; the helper is excluded from
  regional tasks while travelling and resumes the local graph on arrival.
  Journey state carries the origin/target landmark, route points, index and
  bridge usage.
- **Route preference and blocked state** — returning mid-journey reverses the
  traversed outbound route when possible, otherwise re-searches from the current
  world position. A missing route leaves the helper visibly waiting as
  "No safe route" with periodic retries; no teleport or unreachable grant.
- **Save/load** — travelling helpers persist target region, origin, world
  position and world destination; the route is recomputed on load and the walk
  continues. Local worker positions remain valid for legacy saves.
- **World rendering** — all helpers are rendered in one world-space host with
  stable `WorldPosition` values, including Reedwater locals and travellers
  during origin rebasing. Nameplates show Travelling or No safe route.
- **Gate and bridge routes** — the garden east/west/north/south gates and
  Reedwater's west gate are route waypoints; river crossings use the exact
  generated bridge deck points, so helpers stay on visible geometry.
- **Probe hooks** — landmark entry instrumentation, worker world position,
  travelling/region/route/bridge getters and an instrumented journey start.
