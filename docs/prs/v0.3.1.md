## H0 — Save integrity and baseline hardening

Finding F1: the in-game New Garden action called `StartNew()` directly. Its
next autosave could replace the previous garden without a verified archive.

### What changed

- `WorldSaveService.PrepareNewGarden` saves the live session, then archives it
  as one workflow shared by the title screen and the in-game action.
- `WorldSaveService.ArchiveExisting` stages and verifies the manifest and every
  chunk record before publication. The archive manifest and chunk directory are
  swapped as one rollback-capable transaction; a failure restores the previous
  verified archive and leaves the live garden untouched.
- `WorldSaveStore.StageChunkArchive` copies each live record, verifies the copy,
  and stamps the owning world seed. `InstallStagedChunkArchive` publishes only
  verified staging bytes. `RetireLiveChunkChanges` retires the live directory by
  atomic rename and rolls back if the rename fails.
- `RestoreArchive` restores the archived manifest and chunk records as one
  payload. Chunk records from another world seed are ignored; a single
  unreadable archived record cannot make the previous garden unusable.
- `LoadArchive` stays read-only for inspection and title-screen evidence.
- The in-game New Garden path in `GardenClearing.RestartNewGame` now requires
  confirmation, saves and archives, refuses to start if any stage fails, and
  starts the replacement only after success.
- The archive is never claimed unless the published manifest verifies.
